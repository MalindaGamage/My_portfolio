What I focus on

I work best on software that sits close to real operational value: backend services, API platforms, embedded integrations, and automation that removes friction from day-to-day business workflows.

My strongest technical areas are Java, C++, Python, Spring Boot, WSO2, and integration-heavy systems that require careful design and disciplined implementation.

How I work

I prefer clear requirements, direct collaboration, and solutions that stay maintainable after delivery. That means writing readable code, validating edge cases, and keeping architecture practical rather than over-engineered.

If you are hiring for a backend or platform-focused role, this page and the main portfolio are intended to give you a quick and credible picture of how I think and what I build.
